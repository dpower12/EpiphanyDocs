Epiphany utilizes REPENTOGON's system for adding [custom item tags](https://repentogon.com/xml/items.html#customtags) to mark specific items for specific mechanics inside Epiphany, most of which being blacklists. This page features details for all of Epiphany's custom tags, with links to the [official Epiphany wiki](https://tboiepiphany.wiki.gg/wiki/TBOI:_The_Epiphany_Mod_Wiki) that contain a full list of vanilla and Epiphany items that have these tags.

| string ID | Lua table location | Wiki Link | Description | Conditions |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
|noactivesack|Epiphany<br>Item<br>ACTIVE_SACK<br>TAG_NO_ACTIVE_SACK | [Link](https://tboiepiphany.wiki.gg/wiki/Item_Tags_noactivesack) | Not dropped by [Active Sack](https://tboiepiphany.wiki.gg/wiki/Active_Sack) | Has issues or is useless when used as a one-time-use pocket active item. Active Sack already excludes any items with a tag that contributes towards a transformation|
|nogoldenitem|Epiphany<br>Pickup<br>GOLDEN_ITEM<br>TAG_NO_GOLDEN_ITEM | [Link](https://tboiepiphany.wiki.gg/wiki/Item_Tags_nogoldenitem) | Has no additional effects as a [Golden Item](https://tboiepiphany.wiki.gg/wiki/Golden_Item) and cannot naturally spawn as one. | Active items that do nothing, or would have issues, if it would activate twice. Golden Item activations also pass `UseFlag.USE_CARBATTERY` which can be blocked my mods|
|norandomuse|Epiphany<br>Card<br>DRAWN_CARD<br>TAG_NO_RANDOM_USE| [Link](https://tboiepiphany.wiki.gg/wiki/Item_Tags_norandomuse) | Cannot be chosen at random by other effects. Used by [Drawn Card](https://tboiepiphany.wiki.gg/wiki/Drawn_Card) or [Glitch Machine](https://tboiepiphany.wiki.gg/wiki/Beggars_and_Machines#Glitch_Machine) | Active items considered useless or unfair when used at random without any action from the player. Notably ones that damage the player or reroll their inventory|
